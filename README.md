# THE DAILY ATLAS — Thursday 19 November 2026

A one-page picture of the world's news on Thursday 19 November 2026. The page carries 40 verified Thursday stories: 15 illustrated cards lead the layout, a BY THE NUMBERS strip pulls out the day's freshest figures (Anthropic's ANT closing at $97.17, up 17% on its debut; the Dow down 1,101; the ICJ's 9–6 genocide ruling against Myanmar; Walmart's $188.8bn revenue), and a dotted world map below carries a numbered pin for every story, so each item on the page can be located at a glance. The day's biggest developments are all here: the first-ever Xi–Trump–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen and the Peace Declaration they signed, APEC's Shenzhen Vision, the Berlin Coalition of the Willing with the UK's £260m drone package, a Houthi missile hitting Riyadh's PIF Tower, the COP31 draft dropping all fossil-fuel language, GTA VI launching worldwide, the Crimean Bridge hit for a third time in a week, and Wall Street sinking on the Dow's 1,101-point drop. Every story was reported on the day itself and is drawn from wire and agency reporting — sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC and others — and each story was confirmed against two independent sources before it earned its place on the page.

## How it works

The page itself (`entry.png`, 1024×1024) was composed programmatically with PIL. The entry program, `main.py`, simply publishes it: it checks that the bundled image is present and is a PNG, then copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, creating `/output` if needed, and verifies the copy matches the original by size and sha256. Running it repeatedly produces a byte-identical output. Python 3.13, standard library only, no network access.
